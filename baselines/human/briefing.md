## The task

Each report describes one place in California City, CA: a house, a pool, an intersection, a
parking lot. Find the place on the aerial imagery, click it, and press **Commit**. An answer
is correct when your point is on the place the report describes. You have one {MINUTES}-minute
session: find as many places correctly as you can. Speed and accuracy both count.

## Rules

- Work the reports in the order the console gives them.
- Use only the console. No other maps, websites or phones during a session.
- The place may be anywhere on the map. Outlines on the imagery are a guide, not a list of
  answers.
- Tan areas of the map have no imagery.

**Your reports** lists the session's reports in order: "open" is the one you are on,
"answered" or "skipped" are done, and the rest wait. The list is longer than anyone can
finish: answer as many as you can, in order.

## Your session: alone

You search every report yourself, as in practice. A skip counts as a wrong answer.

## Your session: with MAPS

MAPS is an automated search system that has searched the same reports ahead of you. MAPS is
sometimes wrong.

- **MAPS has answered** (its answer opens on the map): check it. Press **Confirm** (Enter) if
  it is right. If not, click the right place and press **Pick another** (S). The place you
  pick must be one MAPS could still have reached; when it is too far, the console says so.
- **MAPS is still searching:** search that report yourself.
- **A skip** lets MAPS's answer count for that report.
- MAPS finishes reports in no fixed order, so a later report may be ready while the open one
  is not. In **Your reports**, the rest read "not started", "MAPS searching" or "result ready".
  You always work on the open report.
