# Briefing: the search study

Read before your first session.

## What you will do

You will read short search reports, each describing one place in California City, CA:
a house, a pool, an intersection, a parking lot. For each report, find the place on the
aerial imagery and put a point on it. An answer is correct when your point is on the place
the report describes.

You work two 40-minute sessions, with a short break between them:

- **Alone.** You search every report yourself.
- **With MAPS.** MAPS is an automated search system that has searched the same kind of
  reports ahead of you. When MAPS has answered a report before you reach it, you check its
  answer: confirm it if it is right, or pick the right place if it is not. When MAPS has
  not answered yet, you search that report yourself. MAPS is sometimes wrong.

In both sessions, find as many places correctly as you can in the 40 minutes. Speed and
accuracy both count.

## Rules

- Work the reports in the order the console gives them. You may skip a report, but a skip
  counts as a wrong answer.
- Use only the console. No other maps, websites or phones during a session.
- The place may be anywhere on the map. Outlines on the imagery are a guide, not a list of
  answers.
- Once you answer, the next report opens. You cannot go back.

## The console

- **The report** is at the top left. The answer buttons are at the bottom of that column.
- **The map** is the collected aerial imagery of the area, north up. Tan areas have no
  imagery.
- **Search box** (top left of the map):
  - Type a street or place name, then pick a result. Every piece of that street lights up.
  - The arrows step along a long street piece by piece.
  - Type two names joined by `&` (for example `Lime Avenue & 88th Street`) to find where
    they cross.
  - Type a kind of place (`parking`, `park`, `pool`) to list the nearest ones.
- **Street map** (the chip, or the O key) shows the map's streets, buildings and their names.
  Hovering over a street shows its name and which way it runs.
- **Measure:** hold Shift and click two points to measure a distance. Esc clears it.
- **Your answer:** click the map where the place is, then press **Commit** (or the S key).
- **Skip this report** is under the answer buttons.

With MAPS, a report MAPS has already answered opens with that answer on the map and why MAPS
chose it:

- **Go to MAPS's answer** (or the M key) brings the map back to MAPS's answer.
- Places MAPS names in its reasoning are marked in colour, each drawn on the map in the same
  colour. Point at one to see it on the map; click it to go there.
- Press **Confirm** (or Enter) to accept MAPS's answer.
- Or click the right place and press **Pick another** (or S). The place you pick must be one
  MAPS could still have reached; when it is too far, the console says so. Choose a place
  nearer MAPS's answer, or press Confirm.

The four practice reports before the first session do not count. You search them alone, with
no MAPS answers: use them to try the search box, the street map and Measure. Practice ends by
itself after the fourth report; **Done practicing** at the top of the console ends it early.
