THE SEARCH STUDY

You need: a Mac with macOS 12 or later (macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon), a
computer with 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, or a 64-bit Linux computer (Ubuntu 20.04
or later, or similar), 8 GB of memory, 6 GB of free disk space, a
mouse, Chrome or Firefox, an internet connection for the first start, about 2
hours, and the participant label (p01, p02, ...) the study lead sent you. Your
label is the only thing that identifies you; never type your name anywhere.

A report is a short text that describes one place in California City, CA. In
the study you find each report's place on an aerial photo.

0. Download and unzip

   Download MAPS-study.zip from the link (about 2.5 GB). Google Drive says the
   file is too large to preview and that it cannot scan it for viruses. Both
   are normal: press "Download anyway".

   Mac:     Double-click the zip if your browser has not unpacked it. Open the
            new "MAPS-study" folder.

   Windows: Right-click the zip, choose Properties, tick "Unblock" at the
            bottom if it is there, and press OK. Right-click the zip again,
            choose "Extract All", then "Extract". Unpacking takes several
            minutes. Open the new "MAPS-study" folder. If it holds only
            another "MAPS-study" folder, open that one.

   Linux:   Right-click the zip and choose "Extract Here", or run
            unzip MAPS-study.zip in a terminal.

1. Start the study

   Mac:     Double-click "Start study (Mac)". The first time, the Mac refuses
            to open it: press Done, Cancel or OK, never "Move to Trash".
            Within the hour, open System Settings > Privacy & Security (on
            macOS 12: System Preferences > Security & Privacy > General),
            scroll down, and press "Open Anyway" next to "Start study (Mac)".
            Enter your Mac password if asked, then press "Open Anyway" or
            "Open". If the Mac asks whether Terminal may use a folder, press
            Allow.

   Windows: Double-click "Start study (Windows)". If a blue "Windows protected
            your PC" box appears, press "More info", then "Run anyway".

   Linux:   Open a terminal in the "MAPS-study" folder (in the file manager,
            right-click inside the folder and choose "Open in Terminal"),
            type sh "Start study (Linux).sh" and press Enter.

   A window with text opens. The first start downloads what the study needs:
   lines of text scroll by for several minutes. Then your browser opens the
   study page. Later starts take seconds and need no internet.

   Use Chrome or Firefox. If the page opened in another browser, copy its
   address, http://127.0.0.1:8788/, into Chrome or Firefox.

   Keep the text window open until you finish. Closing it stops the study.
   When you finish and close it, a Mac may ask whether to terminate the
   running process: press "Terminate".

2. Follow the page

   Type your label. The page then walks you through everything: the briefing,
   four practice reports, session 1 (40 minutes), a 5-minute break, and
   session 2 (40 minutes). Each session's 40 minutes start when you press its
   button, so start it only when you are seated and ready for 40 minutes
   without interruptions. During a session use only the study page: no other
   maps, websites or phones.

   If only the browser closed, open http://127.0.0.1:8788/ in Chrome or
   Firefox. If the text window closed or the computer restarted, start the
   study again as in step 1. The page picks up where you were. A session's
   clock keeps running while the study is stopped.

3. Send your results

   When you finish, the page shows a "Save my results file" button. Your
   browser saves maps-study-<your label>-<a code>.json (the page shows its
   name), usually in your Downloads folder. Email that file to {CONTACT}.
   It holds your answers and their times, nothing else.
