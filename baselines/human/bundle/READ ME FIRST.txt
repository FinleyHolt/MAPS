THE SEARCH STUDY

You need: a Mac or Windows computer, a mouse, Chrome or Firefox, an internet
connection for the first start, about 2 hours, and the participant label (p01,
p02, ...) the study lead sent you. Your label is the only thing that identifies
you; never type your name anywhere.

1. Start the study

   Mac:     Right-click (or Control-click) "Start study (Mac)" and choose Open.
            If the Mac says it cannot check the file, choose Open again. If it
            only offers "Done", open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
            scroll down, press "Open Anyway", and try again.

   Windows: Double-click "Start study (Windows)". If a blue "Windows protected
            your PC" box appears, press "More info", then "Run anyway".

   A window with text opens. The first time, it sets up for a few minutes.
   Then your browser opens the study's page. If it does not, open
   http://127.0.0.1:8788/ in your browser.

   Keep that text window open until you finish. Closing it stops the study.

2. Follow the page

   Type your label. The page then walks you through everything: the briefing,
   four practice reports, session 1 (40 minutes), a 5-minute break, and
   session 2 (40 minutes). Each session's 40 minutes start when you press its
   button, so start it only when you are seated and ready for 40 minutes
   without interruptions. During a session use only the study page: no other
   maps, websites or phones.

   If anything stops (the browser closes, the computer sleeps), start the
   study again as in step 1. It picks up where you were. A session's clock
   keeps running while it is stopped.

3. Send your results

   When you finish, the page offers your results file. Save it and email it to
   the study lead. It holds your answers and their times, nothing else.

Questions about the page or the setup go to the study lead or Ryan Lutz.
Nobody can answer questions about a report.
